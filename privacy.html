Slow Maps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Slow Maps is a short-video channel about real borders on real maps, published as @slowmapstv on YouTube, on Instagram and on its Facebook Page. This policy explains how Slow Maps ("we", "us") handles information.

The short version: we have no app or website you sign in to, and we collect no personal data about anyone. Our publishing tool only posts our own videos to our own channel, Page and account.

1. Our publishing tool

We post our videos through an internal publishing tool that uses YouTube API Services and Meta's Graph API. It acts only on our own YouTube channel, our own Facebook Page and our own Instagram account: it uploads our own videos and their captions, and reads our own public statistics. It does not access, collect or store any data about other users.

2. When you watch or comment

When you watch, like, comment on or follow our posts, the platform you use handles your data under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service for YouTube, and the Meta Privacy Policy for Facebook and Instagram. We see what any account owner sees on their own posts, such as public comments, and we do not copy or keep it anywhere else.

3. Data deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Anything you did on our posts (a like, a comment, a follow) is held by the platform and can be removed in your own YouTube, Facebook or Instagram settings. For any other request, email us at the address below and we will answer.

4. Children

Slow Maps is made for a general audience. We collect no personal information from anyone, children included.

5. Changes to this policy

If anything changes in a way that affects this policy, the change will appear here with a new "Last updated" date.
